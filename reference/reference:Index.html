Step 4

Programming · Daily Structure

Built on time, not guesswork. 5-min warm-up and 5-min coordination open every day. Blocks 1–2 are your mains (1 main + 3 accessories); any extra blocks run light — arms, core, stretch, no heavy main. "Both" days go 2 lifting blocks → conditioning. The finisher flexes 5–10 min to close out, coach's choice. Across the 20-week block, your 1–2 main lifts (marked ▲) progress as strength work — low reps (3–5), a change every 4 weeks, sets first then reps, never past 5 — while accessories hold a steady working prescription. New exercises load in at the next 20-week block.
